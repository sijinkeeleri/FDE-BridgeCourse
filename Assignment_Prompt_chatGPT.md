# Practice Exercise: Crafting Prompts for Different Scenarios

## Scenario

You are a content creator launching a new travel blog called **"WanderMore."** Your goal is to use a generative AI assistant to quickly create different types of content using effective prompting techniques.

---

# 1. Instructional Prompt

### Goal

Get a simple, bulleted list of the **top 5 largest cities in the world by population**.

### Prompt

```text
List the top 5 largest cities in the world by population. Present the answer as a bulleted list. Include the city name, country, and approximate population. Keep the response concise and factually accurate.
```

---

# 2. Role-Based Prompt

### Goal

Generate a warm and inviting introduction for the **About Us** page of the travel blog **WanderMore** from the perspective of a seasoned world traveler.

### Prompt

```text
Act as a seasoned world traveler and professional travel writer. Write a warm, inspiring, and welcoming introduction for the "About Us" page of my travel blog, "WanderMore." Use a friendly and conversational tone that encourages readers to explore new destinations, embrace different cultures, and create unforgettable memories. Keep the introduction between 180–220 words.
```

---

# 3. Zero-Shot Prompt

### Goal

Generate a short creative poem about watching a sunset from a new city.

### Prompt

```text
Write a short, creative poem about the feeling of watching a sunset from a new city.
```

---

# 4. Few-Shot Prompt

### Goal

Teach the AI a custom format for generating traveler tips.

### Prompt

```text
Generate traveler tips using the following format.

Example 1:
Tip: Carry a reusable water bottle. - Why: It saves money and helps reduce plastic waste.

Example 2:
Tip: Learn a few local phrases before your trip. - Why: It helps you connect with locals and shows respect for their culture.

Example 3:
Tip: Keep digital copies of your important documents. - Why: They can be useful if the originals are lost or stolen.

Now generate 5 new traveler tips using the exact same format.
```

---

# Summary

| Prompt Type              | Purpose                                      | Example Goal                                        |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| **Instructional Prompt** | Directly instruct the AI to perform a task   | List the top 5 largest cities by population         |
| **Role-Based Prompt**    | Assign the AI a specific persona             | Write an "About Us" page as a seasoned traveler     |
| **Zero-Shot Prompt**     | Ask the AI without giving examples           | Write a poem about a sunset in a new city           |
| **Few-Shot Prompt**      | Provide examples to teach the desired format | Generate traveler tips following a specific pattern |
